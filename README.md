## Hi, I'm Sam 👋

**Thoughtful interfaces, built for everyone.**

I'm a Senior Front-End Engineer who builds intuitive, accessible web applications with React and TypeScript.

I believe the best interfaces are the ones people don't have to think about. They feel obvious, they respond the way you expect, and they work just as well with a keyboard or a screen reader as with a mouse. Accessibility isn't a checklist I run through at the end of a project; it shapes how I design components, structure markup and write tests from the start.

Much of my work sits where design and engineering meet. I enjoy building design systems and reusable components with clear, predictable APIs that help teams ship consistent, high-quality experiences faster. I also care about the craft behind the scenes: well-typed code, thorough testing and documentation that makes it easy for others to pick things up.
